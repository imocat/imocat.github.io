FAQ

Good questions.

Where do deleted photos go?

To Recently Deleted in the Photos app. iOS keeps them there for up to 30 days.

Does it work with iCloud Photos?

Yes. Deletions sync to your other devices using the same iCloud Photos library.

Does Between Frames upload my photos?

No. Everything is processed on your iPhone.

Limited photo access?

It works with the photos you select. Change access any time.

Can I start over?

Reset review progress for All, Photos or Videos. Deletion history is kept.
