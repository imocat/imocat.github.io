01 · How it works

Three gestures. One confirmation.

Nothing is deleted while you browse. Marking and deleting are two separate steps, and iOS has the final word.

swipe ←

Swipe left to keep

Anything you don't mark stays. There is no Keep button to tap.

swipe ↑

Swipe up to mark

The photo falls into the Dynamic Island. Undo brings it right back.

end of group

One last look

Change your mind on any marked item. Then iOS asks you once to confirm.

# what happens to each item
unreviewed → in this group → kept (default)
→ marked → one last look → iOS confirms → Recently Deleted
02 · Principles

Built for finishing, not for guilt.

No scary counter of everything left. Just the group in front of you.

Small groups

Fifteen items at a time. Leave mid-group and pick up exactly where you stopped.

Videos too

A full-screen vertical feed with sound, favorites and undo. Unmarked videos are kept.

Done stays done

Reviewed items don't sneak back after a restart or a library refresh. Start over only when you choose to.

Focused review

Go straight to Screenshots, a single date, or one album.

Progress

See what you've reviewed and your potential storage savings, split by photos and videos.

03 · Privacy

Nothing leaves your iPhone.

Your photos, videos and review progress are processed on the device. No account, no ads, no analytics. Purchases are handled by Apple and our subscription provider. Read the Privacy Policy.

05 · FAQ

Questions

Where do deleted photos go?

To Recently Deleted in the Photos app. iOS keeps them there for up to 30 days, so you can still recover them.

Does it work with iCloud Photos?

Yes. Deletions sync to your other devices signed in to the same iCloud Photos library.

Does Between Frames upload my photos?

No. Photos and videos stay on your device and are never sent to us.

What if I only allow access to selected photos?

It works with your selection. You can change access any time from Progress or iOS Settings.

Can I start over?

Reset review progress for All, Photos or Videos. Your deletion history is kept.
