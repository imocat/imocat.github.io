FAQ

Good questions.

Where do deleted photos go?

To Recently Deleted in the Photos app. iOS keeps them there for up to 30 days, so you can still recover them.

Does it work with iCloud Photos?

Yes. When you delete items, the change syncs to your other devices that use the same iCloud Photos library.

Does Between Frames upload my photos?

No. Your photos and videos are processed on your iPhone and are never sent to us.

What if I only allow access to selected photos?

Between Frames works with the photos you choose. You can change access anytime in Progress or in iOS Settings.

Can I start over?

Yes. Reset review progress for All, Photos or Videos in Progress. Your deletion history and today's group count are kept.

How do I cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.
