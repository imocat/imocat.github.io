1. Who we are

Between Frames (the "app") is an iPhone app made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app and this website handle and why. If you have a question, email hello@txxy.in.

2. Your photos and videos

To help you review your library, the app asks for access to your photos through Apple's Photos framework. You can allow full access or choose only selected photos. With that access the app reads, on your device only:

  • Your photos, screenshots and videos, and their thumbnails, so you can view them.
  • Metadata such as capture date and time, camera model, exposure settings, file name, resolution, file size and, when present, location. These appear in the Details view and are used to estimate potential storage savings.
  • Your albums, so you can review or add to them.

The app does not copy your media to any server, and we never receive your photos, videos, file names, locations or thumbnails.

Favorites, albums and deletion. When you favorite a photo or add it to an album, the app asks Photos to make that change. When you finish a group and choose to delete items, the app asks iOS to delete them, and iOS shows its own confirmation. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs these changes to your other devices. Apple's handling of your library is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app stores the following locally on your iPhone:

  • Identifiers of items you have reviewed, and your unfinished photo and video groups, including their order, position and marked items.
  • Review history, such as how many items you have viewed and deleted, and estimated storage savings.
  • Settings, such as your group size and the number of new groups started today.

This information is not sent to us. It may be included in your device backup (for example, an iCloud or computer backup) under Apple's terms. Deleting the app removes it from your device.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Between Frames Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes your payment. We never see your name, Apple Account email, or payment details.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to validate purchases, restore them and tell the app whether Pro is active. When the app starts or you make or restore a purchase, the RevenueCat software in the app sends RevenueCat:

  • A random app user identifier created by RevenueCat (not linked to your name or Apple Account).
  • Your App Store transaction and receipt information, such as product, purchase date, renewal status, price and currency, and storefront country.
  • Technical information such as app version, iOS version, device model, language and region settings, and IP address.

We and RevenueCat use purchase history to provide, verify and restore Pro access, support you, and understand purchase and subscription performance. We do not send your photos or videos for these purposes. RevenueCat processes purchase information for us as a service provider. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. Information about your subscription may also be available to us in App Store Connect, in aggregated or transaction form, under Apple's terms.

5. Maps

If a photo includes a location, the Details view can show it on a map provided by Apple MapKit. To draw the map, your device requests map data for that area from Apple. We do not receive this location. Apple's use is covered by its privacy policy.

6. What we don't do

  • We do not include advertising, usage analytics or tracking software. RevenueCat provides purchase analytics as described above.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • We do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under California law.
  • We do not use your photos to train artificial intelligence or for any purpose other than showing them to you in the app.

7. Retention and deletion

Information stored on your device stays there until you reset your review progress, delete the app, or erase your device. Purchase records held by RevenueCat are kept while needed to provide your purchases and as required for accounting and legal purposes. To ask us to delete the RevenueCat record associated with your app installation, email us; you may need to share the app user ID shown with your request, which we will explain when you contact us.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Photo access. Change or remove access anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Between Frames, or choose which photos to share.
  • Review progress. Reset it in the app under Progress → Reset review progress.
  • Subscriptions. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Depending on where you live, including California, the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you may have the right to know, access, correct or delete personal information about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. Because we hold very little information, most requests are about purchase records. Email hello@txxy.in and we will respond within the time required by law. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, we process purchase information to perform our contract with you (providing Pro) and for our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and supporting customers. RevenueCat may process information in the United States and uses appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

9. Children

Between Frames is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

10. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. Like any website, our hosting provider may process standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver and secure the site.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we will give notice in the app or on this website.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: hello@txxy.in.
