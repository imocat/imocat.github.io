Contact us

Questions, bugs, or feature requests? Email hello@txxy.in. Earmark is made by txxy.

To help us understand an issue, include your iPhone and Apple Watch models, iOS and watchOS versions, the Earmark version, what happened, and what you expected. Share steps to reproduce or a screenshot with personal information removed. Never send your API key. We cannot see your library or retrieve recordings remotely.

Frequently asked questions

Is Earmark a subscription?

No. It is a one-time paid download: US $4.99 in the US App Store, with local pricing elsewhere. Optional AI provider API usage is billed separately by your provider. Apple handles purchases and refunds.

Which devices do I need?

Earmark requires an Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later and a paired iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Only the watch records all day. The iPhone receives audio, transcribes it on-device, and provides browsing, search, and Ask.

Recording stopped while my watch was charging. What now?

Recording runs while the Earmark watch recording session is active. After taking the watch off its charger, open Earmark on the watch and check that recording is on. If it stopped, start a new recording session.

Why haven’t recordings appeared on iPhone?

The watch sends 5-minute audio segments when your paired iPhone is nearby. Open Earmark on iPhone to help transfers complete. Tap the watch status pill in Days or open the Apple Watch section in Settings to request a sync. If the watch is not reachable, wake it and open Earmark there.

How do I set up AI?

In Settings, select OpenAI, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, MiniMax, or OpenRouter and add your own API key and model. Ask needs a model that supports streaming tool calls. Your provider’s API access and billing are separate from any consumer chat subscription.

Can I use it without AI?

Yes. Watch recording, transfer, on-device transcription, browsing, and full-text search work without AI. Ask and AI summaries require your own configured provider. Without AI, local key sentences and watch marks remain available.

When are summaries generated?

Automatic hourly summaries are on by default after you configure an AI provider. A finished hour is sent after transcription completes; this can use API credits. Turn the setting off to stop automatic requests. Full day summaries are generated only when you choose to generate one.

Does the iPhone microphone record all day?

No. It is used only while you hold the Ask input to dictate a question; VoiceOver provides a start/stop control. Speech is transcribed on-device, its audio is never stored, and releasing sends the recognized text. Slide up to cancel. Questions are limited to 60 seconds; reaching the limit sends the text.

Can I still read a transcript after its audio expires?

Yes. Audio is automatically cleaned up after your selected retention period, 30 days by default. Transcripts, highlights, summaries, and conversations remain until you delete them. A citation can still locate retained text, but expired audio cannot be replayed.

Do I need to connect Apple Health?

No. It is optional and read-only. With permission, heart rate, workouts, sleep, and steps can appear inside Earmark. This data is never sent to AI. You can disable the connection or revoke access in Apple’s settings.

When will it be available in mainland China?

Earmark is launching outside mainland China first. Mainland China will follow after the required app filing is complete. We have not announced a date.

Troubleshooting

Recording or transfer is stuck

  • Check that recording is active in the watch app and that microphone access is allowed.
  • Keep your paired iPhone nearby, open Earmark on both devices, and allow time for the current 5-minute segment to finish.
  • Use the watch status panel in Days or the Apple Watch section in Settings to request a sync. Check that both devices have enough battery and free storage.

Audio arrived, but text is missing

  • Open Earmark on iPhone and check the transcription status. Silent segments are intentionally skipped.
  • Check the transcription language in Settings. A change applies to newly imported segments.
  • If a speech model is downloading, allow the download to complete with an internet connection and enough free space. Transcription itself runs on-device.

AI answers or summaries fail

  • Check your provider, HTTPS base URL, model, API key, connection, and provider balance or rate limits.
  • For Ask, select a model with streaming tool-call support. A consumer chat subscription does not necessarily include API access.
  • Check that the day has transcribed content. An hourly summary waits until the hour ends and its transcription completes.
  • You can turn off automatic hourly summaries while you investigate. Never include your API key in a support message.

Voice questions do not start

Allow microphone access for Earmark in iPhone settings. With the keyboard dismissed and the input unfocused, hold the Ask input to dictate; a short tap opens typing. Release to send or slide up to cancel. VoiceOver offers a start/stop control. A configured AI provider is required to send questions.

A citation no longer plays

Check whether the source audio has passed its retention period. Retained text can still be opened, but deleted audio cannot be recovered by us. For new recordings, review your retention setting.

Need to clear the library?

Use full deletion in Settings. It removes the iPhone library and keeps API keys by default, with an option to remove them. Stop recording on your watch separately. Read the deletion details before continuing; deletion may be irreversible.

Privacy and terms

Your library stays on your devices. We have no app servers and no remote access to your recordings. Optional AI requests go directly to the provider you configure.

Read the Privacy Policy for data handling and deletion, and the Terms of Use for recording responsibility, purchases, and third-party services.
