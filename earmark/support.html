FAQ

Recording stopped when my watch was on the charger. Recording runs while the Earmark watch app session is active. Check that recording is on after taking the watch off the charger.

Recordings haven't appeared on my iPhone yet. The watch sends audio in 5-minute segments when your iPhone is nearby. Open Earmark on your iPhone to speed this up.

How do I set up AI? In Settings, choose a provider and paste your own API key.
