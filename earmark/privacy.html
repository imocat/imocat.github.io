The short version

Your day stays with you. Earmark 耳记 is developed by txxy. We operate no Earmark servers and do not collect, receive, or store your app data. There is no Earmark account.

Audio and transcripts stay on your devices. Optional AI features send text directly from your iPhone to an AI provider you configure with your own API key. We never receive that text, and the app never sends audio or Apple Health data to AI.

This policy covers Earmark on iPhone and Apple Watch. The separate “This website and support email” section explains visits to this static site and messages you choose to send us.

Data we do not collect

Earmark does not send us your recordings, transcripts, watch marks, search history, AI questions or answers, health information, API keys, or device identifiers. We do not build user profiles, sell your app data, or use it to train models.

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. There is no developer-operated cloud storage or iCloud sync for your Earmark library.

Data on your devices

  • Audio and transfer. Apple Watch records in 5-minute segments. Audio and moment marks transfer directly to your paired iPhone using Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. The watch may keep pending recordings until transfer completes; transferred audio is removed from its queue. The iPhone stores received audio locally.
  • Transcripts and library. Your iPhone skips silent segments and uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition to transcribe speech. Transcripts, timestamps, highlights, a local search index, summaries, and AI conversations are stored locally. No recording is uploaded to txxy.
  • Microphone and speech processing. Watch recording requires microphone access and your action to start. The iPhone microphone is used only to dictate an Ask question while you hold the input; VoiceOver provides a start/stop control. Dictation is transcribed on-device, its audio is never saved, and the microphone stops on release, stop, or cancellation. Sending a question sends its recognized text to your provider. Apple speech models may need an initial download.
  • Health and preferences. With permission, selected Apple Health information is read and displayed only inside Earmark. App settings and AI connection details are local; API keys are kept in the iOS Keychain.

Optional third-party AI

You choose whether to configure AI. Presets include OpenAI, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, MiniMax, and OpenRouter. Requests travel directly from your iPhone to your chosen provider, using your own API key, without an Earmark server in between.

  • Ask: the text of your question, conversation context, and relevant retrieved transcript excerpts, including source timestamps or citation identifiers, are sent to answer your question. Requests also include instructions and limited context such as the device’s date, time zone, and preferred language.
  • Day summaries: when you choose to generate one, relevant transcript text is sent. Longer days may be processed in multiple requests and combined from intermediate text summaries.
  • Automatic hourly summaries: this setting is on by default once you configure a provider. When an hour has ended and its transcription is complete, that hour’s transcript text is sent automatically to create its summary and topics. You can turn this off in Settings.

Original audio and Apple Health data are never sent to AI. Your key authenticates requests to the provider and is stored only in your device’s non-syncing Keychain. The provider receives requests over the internet and may process connection information such as your IP address.

The provider’s own privacy policy, retention practices, terms, and any model-routing arrangements apply to the text it receives. We do not control those practices. Review them before configuring a provider, and avoid sending information you do not want it to process. You control your provider, model, key, and automatic-summary setting; provider usage may incur separate charges. Recording, transcription, browsing, and search do not require AI.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you allow access, Earmark can read heart rate (including resting heart rate), workouts, sleep, and steps to display alongside your day. It requests read access only and never writes to Apple Health.

Health information is temporarily cached in memory for display. It is not added to the transcript search index, sent to AI, shared with txxy or other parties, used for advertising, or sold. Earmark does not transfer it off your iPhone. You can turn off the connection in Earmark and revoke access in Apple’s Health or privacy settings. Deleting Earmark does not delete the original records in Apple Health.

Recording other people

Before recording other people, obtain any required consent and follow the recording and privacy laws that apply where you are. You are responsible for deciding when and where recording is lawful. Do not record where recording is prohibited.

Children

Earmark is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 where applicable local rules require that higher age. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. If you have a concern about a child’s use of Earmark or a message sent to us, contact us at hello@txxy.in.

Your choices and deletion

  • Recording and permissions. Start and stop recording on your watch. You can revoke microphone access in system settings, decline Apple Health access, or stop using voice questions.
  • Audio retention. Audio is automatically removed after the period you choose: 7, 14, 30, or 90 days, or kept indefinitely. The default is 30 days. Cleanup runs when the app processes maintenance, rather than at an exact expiry instant. Text, highlights, summaries, conversations, and their search index remain until you delete them. Expired audio cannot be replayed, but its retained transcript can still be read.
  • Full deletion. Use the full-delete option in Settings to erase the iPhone’s Earmark library, including recordings, transcripts, highlights, summaries, conversations, and the search index. API keys are retained by default; select the option to remove them as well. Watch recording is controlled separately, so stop it on the watch if you do not want new recordings.
  • Uninstalling. Deleting the app removes its locally stored app library. iOS Keychain items can survive uninstalling; use full deletion with key removal before uninstalling if you also want to remove saved API keys. Remove the watch app too if you want to remove its local data.
  • AI and provider data. Turn off automatic hourly summaries or remove your AI configuration to stop future AI requests. Deleting local content does not erase text already received by a provider. Contact that provider or use its controls for any applicable deletion request.

Because we never receive your library, we cannot retrieve, restore, or delete it remotely. Keep anything essential before choosing deletion or a shorter retention period.

Security

Earmark uses the app’s local device storage and stores API keys in the iOS Keychain with device-only, non-syncing protection. AI connections require HTTPS. Keep your devices and operating systems up to date, use a device passcode, and protect your API keys.

No device or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Earmark does not provide a developer-hosted backup, and deleting data may be irreversible. We do not promise a separate end-to-end encryption service or control the security practices of your AI provider.

International users

Earmark is launching outside mainland China first. The app does not send your library to an Earmark server in any country. If you enable AI, your provider may process the text you send in countries other than your own, under its policies and the laws that apply to it. You choose that provider and are responsible for using it lawfully.

Any privacy or consumer rights that apply to you under mandatory local law remain in place. You can exercise control over local app data through your device and Earmark settings; contact us about questions concerning txxy.

This website and support email

This static website is separate from the app. It has no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, account forms, or tracking cookies. It loads the Figtree font from Google Fonts; your browser contacts Google’s font services and the site’s hosting provider, which can receive standard connection information such as your IP address and requested resources. Their infrastructure policies apply. The website does not access your Earmark library.

If you email us for support, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you voluntarily provide so we can respond. This is separate from automatic app data collection. Please do not send recordings, health information, or API keys unless you specifically intend to share them. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Earmark or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be posted here with a revised “Last updated” date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice. Check this page when deciding whether to enable new features.
