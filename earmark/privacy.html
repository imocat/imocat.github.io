Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Earmark is built so that your recordings stay with you. We do not operate any servers and we do not collect, receive, or store any of your data.

What stays on your devices

  • Audio recorded on your Apple Watch is transferred directly to your paired iPhone and stored only there.
  • Transcripts are produced on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech recognition.
  • Health data (heart rate, activity), if you grant access, is read from Apple Health and shown only inside the app. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Earmark does not sync your data to iCloud.

AI features

AI features are optional and use an AI provider that you configure with your own API key (for example OpenAI, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, or OpenRouter). When you ask a question or generate a summary, the relevant transcript excerpts are sent directly from your iPhone to that provider. That data is handled under your provider's own privacy policy. Your API key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Analytics and tracking

Earmark contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Recording others

You are responsible for obtaining consent from people you record and for complying with the laws where you live.

Deleting your data

Audio is deleted automatically after the retention period you choose (30 days by default). Deleting the app removes all of its data.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 14 日

耳记没有任何服务器，我们不收集、不接收、不存储你的任何数据。

  • 音频：Apple Watch 录制的音频直接传到你配对的 iPhone，只保存在 iPhone 上。
  • 转写：在 iPhone 上使用 Apple 本地语音识别完成。
  • 健康数据：在你授权后，从"健康"App 读取心率和活动数据，仅在 App 内显示，不会发送到任何地方。
  • 耳记不会把数据同步到 iCloud。

AI 功能：AI 功能为可选项，使用你自己配置的服务商和 API Key（例如 OpenAI、DeepSeek、通义、Kimi、OpenRouter）。你提问或生成总结时，相关的转写片段会从你的 iPhone 直接发送到该服务商，并受该服务商的隐私政策约束。API Key 保存在设备的钥匙串中。

统计与追踪：耳记不包含任何统计、广告或追踪 SDK。

录制他人：录制他人前，请取得对方同意，并遵守当地法律。

删除数据：音频会在你设置的保留期限（默认 30 天）后自动删除。删除 App 即会删除其全部数据。
