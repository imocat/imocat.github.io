1. The service

The App is a focus timer presented as a museum visit. You choose a hall and a duration and keep the App in the foreground; when the session completes, the artwork is added to a collection stored on your device. No account is required. Locking the device or leaving the App pauses the session; returning within 30 seconds resumes it, and a longer absence ends it. The in-app “Focus Rules” describe the current rules.
